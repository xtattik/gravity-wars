Rules

How to play

Each player has a ship. Take turns firing at the other ship. Every planet pulls on your shot, so it curves. Hit the enemy ship to win the round.

Angle is measured anticlockwise from the right. 0° is right, 90° is up, 180° is left and 270° is down. This is the same for both players.

Speed (0–100) is how fast the shot leaves your ship.

Planets

Colour shows density: red = low density, then orange, yellow, green, cyan, up to blue = high density. Mass = density × radius³, so size and colour both matter. A big red planet can pull about as hard as a small blue one. Gravity gets weaker with the square of the distance.

Controls

  • Click a box and type a value (decimals like 37.25 are fine), or drag the sliders. Then press FIRE or Enter. Tab jumps from angle to speed.
  • Keyboard: ←/→ change the angle and ↑/↓ change the speed. Hold Shift for steps of 10 (angle) or 5 (speed), or Ctrl for steps of 0.1.
  • While a shot is flying, press Enter or Skip to jump to the end.
  • Your old trails stay on screen, so use them to adjust your next shot.

A shot ends when it hits a planet or a ship, leaves the play area, or burns out: after 25 seconds if it's off-screen, or after 90 seconds even if it's still on screen. You can hit your own ship, so be careful!
